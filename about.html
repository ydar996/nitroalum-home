Built for the Groups That Raised Us

School ends. The class does not. NitroAlum exists because the people we grew up with deserve better tools than a chat thread and a treasurer's notebook.

Why We Built It

Alumni Groups Do Serious Work on Casual Tools

Around the world, class sets and alumni associations collect millions in dues, build classrooms, pay school fees for members' children, and carry members through illness and bereavement. Almost all of it runs on chat threads, personal bank accounts, and memory.

That works until it does not: a treasurer moves on, a donor disputes a figure, a member in need has nowhere private to ask. Groups do not fail from lack of goodwill. They fail from lack of infrastructure.

NitroAlum is the infrastructure: the tools an institution gets, shaped for a community of friends.

What We Believe

  • Transparency is loveGroups fight about money only when they cannot see it. Open ledgers keep friendships intact.
  • Privacy is dignityContact details, hardship cases, and anonymous donations are protected by design, not by promises.
  • Independence is non negotiableEach group is its own world: its officers, its rules, its data. Nobody else's group ever touches it.
  • Memory is infrastructureThe yearbook is not nostalgia. It is the register the whole platform stands on.
How it Stays Honest

Designed So Trust Does Not Depend on People

  • Nothing is quietly deletedMistakes are corrected with visible reversals, not erased. The ledger keeps the whole story.
  • Every action leaves a nameSensitive operations require officers to confirm their identity and are written to an audit trail.
  • Members can verifyPublished financial snapshots and public project pages let the register check the committee's work.
  • No shadow ITThe group's data lives in the platform, not in one officer's phone or inbox.
Who it Serves

Classes, Sets, and Associations

NitroAlum serves secondary school class sets, university alumni chapters, professional associations, and any bonded group that wants to operate like an institution while feeling like a family.

Whether your set is 30 people or 3,000, whether everyone is in one city or scattered across five continents, the platform is the same: one private home with the group's name on the door.
