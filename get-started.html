From a Name on a List to a Living Platform

No procurement, no IT project. A group can go from a chat thread to a full alumni platform in a single sitting.

The Path

Four Steps to Your Own Platform

  • Create the groupOne person fills a short form: school name, category, graduating year, country and state. The platform generates the group's address automatically, something like nitroalum.com/your-school-1995. The founder becomes the first President.
  • Import the registerUpload your class list or yearbook records. Names, sets, hostels, and maiden names come in as member records, ready to be claimed.
  • Seat your officersFill the organogram: President, General Secretary, Financial Secretary, Welfare Officer, and any custom offices or committees your group needs.
  • Activate the membersEach member gets a personal, single use link to set their own password. Send it by email automatically or paste it into your existing chat with them. Their login attaches to their real record, no duplicates.

What Members Can Do Once Inside

  • Keep their own record freshContact details, country, and profile updates apply to their directory entry.
  • See what they owe, pay it, prove itDues balances, payment claims, and receipts in one place.
  • Follow the projectsPublic project pages, thermometers, hostel rankings, and donor honour rolls.
  • Vote and serveElections, nominations, committees, meetings, and announcements reach them directly.
  • Ask for help quietlyHardship appeals go to welfare officers privately, never to the whole register.
