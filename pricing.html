How Plans Work

  • Every plan includes every feature. Plans differ only by the number of active members.
  • Active members means active member records. Deceased members, deleted members, event guests, and children in association households are not counted.
  • Billing is in advance: monthly, quarterly at 3% off, or annual at 9% off.
  • New groups get a 30 day free trial, with no card needed to start.
  • NitroAssoc uses the same plans. The chapter totals view for national bodies is part of Federation.
  • Your group is notified when it passes its plan limit and moves to the right plan at its next renewal, no sooner than 30 days after the notice. Service is never cut off for being over the limit.
