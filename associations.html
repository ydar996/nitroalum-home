What Your Association Gets

Built for How Associations Actually Run

Associations run on dues, chapters, and meetings that must hold up to scrutiny. NitroAssoc is shaped around exactly that.

Mandatory Dues, No Chasing

Dues are not a suggestion here. Billing is automatic, every member sees what they owe, and good standing is tracked so arrears are a fact, not an argument.

Chapters and Branches

City chapters and diaspora branches collect locally while the national body sees one roll up: every remittance reconciled, every chapter's books visible.

Constitution and Bylaws

Your governing documents live in one library with a full amendment history. When a proposal changes the rules, the record shows when and how.

Meetings With a Record

Notices go out on the rules your constitution sets. Attendance, quorum, motions, and minutes are captured so every decision is defensible.

Welfare Schemes and Levies

Benevolent funds, per event levies, and structured support for members in need. Collected fairly, tracked transparently, handled with dignity.

Elections and Compliance

Secret ballot elections for your executive council, audit ready exports for annual returns, and a ledger that answers questions before they are asked.

Why It Feels Different

Members Who Share a Mission

Associations build their structure from scratch: open membership, chapters in different cities, a constitution that actually governs, and dues that fund real work. NitroAssoc was designed for that shape from the start.

NitroAssoc is not a cut down version of anything. It is a purpose built platform already carrying real groups and real money.
