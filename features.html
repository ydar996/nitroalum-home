Every Feature, Built for Real Alumni Groups

NitroAlum is not a contact list with a donate button. It is a complete operating system for an alumni body: people, money, projects, welfare, and governance in one private place.

Member Directory and Yearbook Profiles

Import your register once and it becomes a living directory. Each member owns their profile and keeps their own contact details current.

  • Bulk import of class lists and yearbook records
  • Maiden names preserved, married surnames displayed
  • Graduating set, hostel, nickname, and photos
  • Encrypted contact details per group
  • Personal activation links for every member, no duplicates
  • Members update their own records; executives approve joins

Dues, Payments, and Claims

Run a proper dues register: bill members, record payments, let members submit claims for review, and reconcile against bank statements.

  • Dues obligations per member with partial payments
  • Recorded payments with bank references
  • Member payment claims with approve and query flow
  • Bank statement import and auto matching
  • 40 plus currencies, multi currency groups supported
  • Reversals keep a full audit trail, nothing is deleted

Treasury and Ledger

A double entry ledger under everything. Funds, budgets, outflows, and published snapshots give the committee numbers they can defend.

  • Named funds with independent balances
  • Cost centres with annual budgets and utilisation tracking
  • Outflows with payee, evidence, and approval chain
  • Immutable journal entries with proper reversals
  • Published financial snapshots for members
  • Fund distribution and balances on the dashboard

Projects and Fundraising

Turn class energy into finished projects. Track pledges and receipts per project, rank hostels and houses, and honour donors properly.

  • Project funds with public thermometers
  • Pledges tracked separately until received
  • Hostel and house contribution rankings
  • Anonymous donation display: members see Anonymous, executives see the donor
  • Project updates and public project pages
  • Contribution history per member

Welfare and Hardship Appeals

Members facing hardship can appeal privately. The welfare officer reviews, the committee approves, and the member's dignity stays intact.

  • Private hardship appeals visible only to welfare officers
  • Payee privacy enforced in the ledger itself
  • Welfare fund balances tracked separately
  • Evidence files restricted to authorised reviewers
  • Open appeals surfaced on the executive dashboard

Governance, Elections, and Committees

Run the group like an institution. Offices, elections, committees, and an organogram that reflects how your group is actually structured.

  • Elections with nominations, acceptance, and voting
  • Editable organogram: add, edit, retire, and restore offices
  • Sub committees for special projects and group affairs
  • Capability based permissions per office
  • The first sign up becomes President; offices transfer cleanly
  • Constitution and group documents in one place

Meetings, Announcements, and Messages

Keep the whole group informed: meetings with agendas and minutes, announcements that reach every member, and group messaging.

  • Scheduled meetings with attendance
  • Published announcements to all members
  • Group messaging between members
  • Notifications surfaced in the app
  • The next meeting always visible on the dashboard

Hostels and Houses

Keep the boarding school rivalries alive. Map every member to their house, rank houses by contribution, and let the set pride do the fundraising.

  • Boarding house membership per member
  • House rankings by project contributions
  • Day students tracked distinctly
  • Named contributor lists per house
  • Assign members to hostels in bulk

Executive Dashboard and Reports

One screen for the committee: what needs attention, the financial position, dues collection, membership composition, and activity.

  • Needs Attention queue: approvals, claims, unmatched bank lines
  • Fund balances, distribution, income, spending, net position
  • Cost centre spend against budgets
  • Dues billed, collected, outstanding, collection rate
  • Membership by set, country, gender, and status
  • Period filters: All Time, By Year, Year to Date, Month to Date

Privacy, Security, and Independence

Every group is a private world. Data is isolated per group, sensitive details are encrypted, and every sensitive action is audited.

  • Complete data isolation between groups
  • Encrypted contact details with access logging
  • Step up confirmation for sensitive actions
  • Full audit trail of executive actions
  • Self hosted email, no third party marketing mail
  • Your group owns its data and its rules
