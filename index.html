Why It Changes Things

The Group Chat Was Never Enough

A chat group is where a class talks. A platform is where a class lives. The difference shows up the first time money, memory, or a member in need is involved.

"Where did the money go?" is the question that kills alumni groups. A shared, honest ledger is the answer that keeps them alive.
  • Money everyone can seeDonations, dues, and spending live in a ledger every executive can inspect, with published reports members can read themselves. Trust stops depending on personalities.
  • Memory that outlives the shoeboxYour yearbook becomes a living directory. Maiden names, house affiliations, and photos stay attached to each person, updated as lives change.
  • Help without humiliationA member facing hardship applies privately. Only the welfare officer sees the details. The group helps; the member keeps their dignity.

Built for How Groups Actually Operate

  • Self serve onboardingThe first person who signs up a group becomes its President. No paperwork, no waiting for us.
  • Your own addressEvery group lives at nitroalum.com/your-group. Simple to share, easy to remember.
  • Anonymous donors, honest booksA donor can hide their name from members while executives still see the truth. Generosity and accountability at once.
  • Hostels and housesBoarding house maps, house rankings, and class sets keep the old rivalries alive for the right reasons.
  • Serious about privacyContact details are encrypted per group, access is audited, and no group ever sees another group's data.
Questions

Answers People Ask First

How do members get into the platform?

Each member gets a personal, single use link to set their own password. Executives can send it by email or paste it into the chat you already use. The login attaches to the member's real record, never a duplicate.

Is our group's data private?

Completely. Every group is its own isolated world with its own officers and its own address. Contact details are encrypted, sensitive actions are audited, and no group can see inside another.

Can a donor stay anonymous?

Yes, honestly. Members see the gift listed as Anonymous while executives keep the real donor name for the record. Generosity and accountability at the same time.

Can we run elections and committees?

Yes. Nominations, acceptance, voting, an editable organogram, and sub committees for special projects are all built in. Offices transfer cleanly as terms end.
